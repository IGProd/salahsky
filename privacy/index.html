SalahSky · Prayer Times & Athan

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

SalahSky has no server. There is no account to create, nothing to log in to, and no copy of your data anywhere but on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app remembers — your city, your calculation method, which adhan you chose, your reminders — is written to the app's own storage on the device. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Location

If you allow it, SalahSky reads your device's location to work out your prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. That calculation happens on the phone, with no network involved.

Location is optional. You can search for a city by name instead and never grant the permission at all.

What is sent, and to whom

SalahSky contacts these services directly from your phone. No account, no advertising ID and no device identifier is attached to any of these requests.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
api.open-meteo.com Coordinates of the city being shown Current weather and forecast
geocoding-api.open-meteo.com The city name you type Finding a city to add
api.bigdatacloud.net Coordinates Turning a location into a place name
nominatim.openstreetmap.org Coordinates The same, when the above is unavailable
api.aladhan.com Coordinates and a calculation method Cross-checking prayer times

Each of these has its own privacy policy, and none of them is given anything that identifies you as a person.

Google Play services

  • Location services — the app asks Google Play services for a position fix. The fix is returned to the app and used on the device.
  • In-app updates and the rating prompt — only on installs that came from Google Play. These are Google's own components and SalahSky sends them nothing about you.

What SalahSky does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDK in the app
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No accounts, sign-in, email address or phone number
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera
  • No selling or sharing of anything with anyone
